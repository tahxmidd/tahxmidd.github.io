About

i grew up playing and building on roblox — it's actually what got me into programming in the first place. developing places and trying to make games with lua scripting turned into teaching kids how to make their own scripts and games, and it really opened my mind to all the different paths you can take in tech.

right now, i'm studying it at ucf, interning as a software engineer, and building a platform called pegasus pair to help students at our school make friends. since roblox was the original inspiration for my tech journey, this site is just a little tribute to where it all started.

relevant coursework: computer logic & organization, object-oriented programming (java), security in computing.
